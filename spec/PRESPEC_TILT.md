# Pre-specification: exponential-tilting contamination estimate (frozen before any run)
Date: 2026-11-26. Context: TPC failed (PRESPEC_TPC), audit-budget law failed (PRESPEC_AUDIT). Literature basis: Qin (1998) two-sample density-ratio model; Zhang, Tian & Li (2026, arXiv 2603.21623) exponential tilting restores identifiability under label noise; Yao, Rava, Tong & James (arXiv 2112.00314) label-noise-adjusted NP umbrella.
## Idea
Observed positives L are a sample from F1 (flips only remove positives). Observed negatives U follow g = (1-pi) f0 + pi f1. Assume f1(s) = f0(s) exp(a + b s) for the (logit-scale) score s. Then f1/g = e^z / ((1-pi) + pi e^z), z = a + b s, so pi is identified by the saturation level of the density ratio (1/pi), without assuming irreducibility or stationarity of prevalence. Estimation: maximum likelihood of P(L | s, L u U) = rho h / (1 + rho h), rho = |L|/|U|, over (a, b, pi), pi <= 0.9, standardised s, 2 starts, Nelder-Mead 600 iterations, at most 20,000 points (seeded subsample). Then the CQ threshold is computed with pi_tilt (cq_lib.cq_threshold unchanged).
## Rule
tilt: cq_threshold(neg, posv, alpha, pi_tilt). Fallback to pi_hat (CQ) only if |L| < 20 or |U| < 50. No other guard, grid, or cap.
## Known weaknesses (declared before running)
Simulation (not HPC): exact when scores are Gaussian with equal variance or logit-beta; biased when variances differ (pi 0.079 vs 0.091; acal 1.99 vs CQ 0.39). Expected to fail under delayed labels (labelled positives are older) and strong misspecification.
## Design
Same as PRESPEC_TPC/AUDIT: noise random, delay, delay2; poison 0, 0.2, 0.4; FPR 1% and 0.1%; LR/RF/XGB; BASE, CW. Dev: creditcard, IEEE-CIS, UNSW-NB15. Confirmation (analysed only after dev): Elliptic, CIC-IDS2017.
## Hypotheses (Holm over all tests within a hypothesis, alpha 0.05, paired over units; units share windows so p-values are descriptive)
HX1: tilt lowers absolute calibration vs naive (poison >= 0.2) in a majority of dev dataset x noise x op x poison cells.
HX2: tilt vs CQ: tilt beats CQ (Holm) in at least 25% of dev cells and is not significantly worse in more cells than it is better.
HX3: median |pi_tilt - pi_true| < median |pi_hat - pi_true| on dev, separately for random, delay, delay2.
HX4: HX1 holds on at least one confirmation dataset.
Any failure is reported as such; nothing is retuned.
## Novelty note
Exponential tilting for identifiability and NP error control are published (Qin 1998; Zhang et al. 2026; Yao et al.). The claimed contribution would be its use for fixed-FPR thresholds under one-sided poisoning with explicit failure boundaries, not the tilting model itself.
